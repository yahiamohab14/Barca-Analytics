Dribbling stats

Take-on. WhoScored's "TakeOn" event: a player tries to beat an opponent with the ball. Successful means he got past; failed means the defender won the ball or the ball ran away from him. WhoScored doesn't log carries, so runs with the ball that don't beat an opponent aren't here. What happened next looks at the same possession for 10 seconds after the take-on: led to a shot if the team shot in that window, led to a key pass if a pass that set up a shot was played, and ball lost within 5 s if the opponent took the ball back within 5 seconds.

Dispossessed is when a player lost the ball to a tackle without trying a take-on; miscontrols are unsuccessful touches where the ball got away. Dribbled past counts how often a Barça player was the defender beaten by an opponent's take-on, and stopped how often he won that duel. The defender is the opponent-side event WhoScored logs at the same moment as the take-on. Final third and box use the opponent's final third and penalty area.
