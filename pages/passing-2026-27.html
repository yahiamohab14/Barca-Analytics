How it's built. Every pass Barça made, taken from WhoScored's match-centre event data. Passes leave out throw-ins. Progressive is a completed open-play pass that moves the ball toward the opponent's goal by at least 30 m inside Barça's half, 15 m when it crosses halfway, or 10 m inside the opponent's half. Final third and into box count completed open-play passes that start outside that zone and end inside it. Key passes set up a shot and assists set up a goal, both as tagged by WhoScored and including set pieces. Long balls, crosses and through balls use WhoScored's own tags; crosses exclude corners. Forward % is the share of passes that end at least 5 m further upfield. Received counts completed passes where the player was the next Barça player on the ball. Per-90 figures are shown for players with at least 90 minutes.

The network. Circles sit at the average spot where each player made his passes (nudged apart slightly where they would overlap) and are sized by passes attempted. Lines join players who completed passes to each other, thicker for more (both directions added together). Corners and throw-ins are left out. The single-match network stops at Barça's first substitution by default. The season network adds up every match in the chosen competition and starts with the regulars (players with at least half of the available minutes); lower the slider to bring in the rotation players. When the first sub comes early, the single-match network covers only those minutes, so switch to full match.

Coverage. Barça have played 15 competitive matches in 2026/27 so far. WhoScored has event data for the 11 LaLiga and 4 Champions League matches used here.
