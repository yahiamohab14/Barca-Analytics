How the xG is made. WhoScored doesn't publish xG, so the values here come from our own model: a logistic regression on distance to goal, the angle of the goal mouth, header or foot, big chance, one-on-one, counter-attack, corner or set piece and direct free kick. It was fitted on 3,141 non-penalty shots (by both teams) from Barça and PSG matches and, tested on shots it hadn't seen, ranks goals above misses about 77% of the time, in the same range as public xG models. Penalties are set to 0.76, the usual long-run conversion rate. Numbers will differ a little from Opta, FBref or Understat. Own goals aren't shots, so goal totals here can be slightly below the scoreline, and penalty shoot-outs are left out.

Reading the map. Each circle is one shot, placed where it was taken with the goal at the top. Bigger circles mean higher xG (circle area follows xG). Filled circles are goals; open circles were saved, blocked (dashed), hit the post (gold) or missed the target (faint).
