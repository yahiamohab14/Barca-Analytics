How it's made. WhoScored records where every on-ball action happens, but not where players stand when they are away from the ball. So with the ball uses a player's passes, touches, take-ons, shots and the spots where he received passes, and without the ball uses his tackles, interceptions, recoveries, blocks, clearances, defensive aerials, fouls, the duels where he was dribbled past and the offsides he caught. Team-shape circles sit at each player's median spot (more robust than the average) for players with at least the chosen minutes. Height is the median depth of the outfield players, width the distance between the widest outfield players and length the distance from the deepest to the highest outfield player. This is a reading of where players act, not GPS-style tracking.
